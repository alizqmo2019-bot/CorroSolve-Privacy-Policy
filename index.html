Privacy Policy — CorroSolve Summary: CorroSolve runs fully offline on your device. It does not collect any personal data, does not transmit any data or test results to external servers, and requires no internet connection for any of its features. 1) Scope This policy applies to the CorroSolve app published by Ali ALalama and distributed through the Microsoft Store. 2) Offline-first design The app does not collect any personal data or identifying information. The app does not transmit any data, projects or test results to any external server. The app does not require an internet connection to function. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. 3) Locally stored data All data is stored locally on your device under %LOCALAPPDATA%\CorroSolve\: your projects and corrosion test records (SQLite database), display settings (language, theme, governing standard) in settings.json, and a crash log (crash.log) if a fault occurs. None of this leaves your device, and you can delete it at any time. 4) Exported files When you export a PDF report or an Excel workbook, you choose the destination folder and file name. Those files are saved where you asked, and are never sent anywhere by the app. 5) Permissions The app declares only the runFullTrust capability, which is required for any desktop app packaged as MSIX. No network or internet capability is declared. 6) Children CorroSolve is a professional engineering utility that collects no personal data; it is not directed at children. 7) Changes This policy may be updated with new releases.
