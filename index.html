Privacy Policy for CorroSolve

Publisher: Ali ALalama

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction

CorroSolve is a professional, offline-first engineering calculator designed specifically for metallurgical engineers, materials scientists, and researchers. Built to align with ASTM and NACE standards, it provides a comprehensive suite of tools for corrosion analysis, material evaluation, and data visualization.

2. Data Collection and Security

Strictly operating entirely offline with no network dependencies, CorroSolve ensures that all your sensitive engineering data, sample records, and project files remain 100% locally stored on your device for maximum security.

The application does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, user data, or telemetry data with the publisher or any third parties.

3. Required Permissions

The application only requests the minimum local permissions necessary to function on your Windows system. Because it operates completely offline, it does not access your internet connection, nor does it access your microphone, camera, or location.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to comply with Microsoft Store policies or reflect changes in the app. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact the developer through the Microsoft Store support channels.
